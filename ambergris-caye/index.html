Explore the Island

🏖️ Top Spots to Visit by Golf Cart

Ambergris Caye is best explored at your own pace. Here are the must-see destinations within easy reach.

🏝️

Secret Beach

The island's most popular destination, shallow turquoise water, beach bars, and a laid-back vibe. About a 25-minute cart ride north of town. Go early to beat the crowd.

🐠

Hol Chan Marine Reserve

World-class snorkeling right off the coast, nurse sharks, rays, and reef fish. Book a guided snorkel tour from the water taxi dock in town.

🦈

Shark Ray Alley

Snorkel with friendly nurse sharks and southern stingrays in shallow, calm water. Usually combined with Hol Chan in a half-day tour.

🌅

Downtown San Pedro

Stroll Barrier Reef Drive for local restaurants, souvenir shops, and beach bars. The town is compact and easy to navigate by cart.

🌿

North Ambergris

Cross the Boca del Rio bridge (included in every rental) and head north for quieter beaches, jungle roads, and upscale resorts.

🦆

Boca del Rio Park

A local favorite for sunsets and weekend gatherings. Cross the bridge and you're in a quieter part of the island with stunning lagoon views.

Transportation

🛺 Getting Around San Pedro

Golf carts are the primary mode of transport on Ambergris Caye. Here's what you need to know.

🚗

Golf Carts

The go-to option for exploring the island at your own pace. OJ delivers to your hotel, the airport, or the water taxi, cart is ready when you arrive.

⛽

Fuel & Range

Carts run on gas. Fill up at the gas station near town. A full tank covers the entire island and back with ease, most guests never need to refuel.

🌉

Boca del Rio Bridge

The toll bridge connecting downtown to North Ambergris. The bridge pass is included in every OJ rental, no need to carry extra cash.

✈️

Getting to San Pedro

Fly into Philip Goldson International Airport (BZE) in Belize City, then take a 20-min domestic flight to San Pedro (SPR) via Tropic Air or Maya Island Air, or the water taxi from the marine terminal.

Local Knowledge

💡 Visitor Tips

Straight from locals who live and work on the island.

💵

Currency

The Belize Dollar (BZD) is pegged at 2:1 to USD. Most businesses accept USD directly. Cards are widely accepted in tourist areas.

🌤️

Best Weather

Peak season is December–April with dry skies and calm seas. July is warm and lively with Lobster Fest, some afternoon showers but rarely all-day rain.

🦟

Bug Spray

Pack DEET-based repellent, especially for evenings near the lagoon side. The beach and cart rides have natural breezes that keep most bugs away.

📶

Connectivity

DigiCell and Smart offer local SIM cards. Most hotels and restaurants have Wi-Fi. Coverage is strong in town and decent up north.

🕘

Island Time

Things move at a relaxed pace, embrace it. Book your cart in advance during Lobster Fest and peak season (Dec–Apr) as availability fills up quickly.

🛡️

Insurance

All OJ golf carts include liability insurance. Note: full coverage insurance for golf carts is not available in San Pedro, this is standard across all rental providers on the island.

Common Questions

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Have a question about renting or visiting? Here are the ones we hear most.

What is included in the rental price?

All rentals include tax, liability insurance, the Boca del Rio bridge pass, free delivery and pickup to most locations, and 24/7 roadside assistance. The price you see is the price you pay, no surprise fees at pickup.

Is full coverage insurance available for golf cart rentals?

No, full coverage insurance for golf carts is not available in San Pedro, Belize. This is standard across all rental providers on the island. All OJ rentals include liability insurance as required.

How much does a golf cart rental cost?

4-person carts start at $45 USD/24hr. 6-person carts are $75 USD/24hr. Decoration add-ons (birthdays, bachelorettes, anniversaries) are $40 USD extra. Multi-day discounts are available, WhatsApp us for a quote.

Do I need a driver's license?

A valid driver's license is recommended but not required to rent. You must be at least 18 years old to operate a golf cart in San Pedro.

When is the best time to visit San Pedro?

December through April is peak season with the driest weather. July is fantastic for Lobster Fest. The island is beautiful year-round, shoulder months (May–June, September–November) offer great value with fewer crowds.

How do I get from Belize City to San Pedro?

From Philip Goldson International Airport (BZE), take a 20-minute domestic flight with Tropic Air or Maya Island Air to San Pedro (SPR). Alternatively, take a taxi to the marine terminal and board the Belize Express or San Pedro Belize Express water taxi, about 75 minutes.
